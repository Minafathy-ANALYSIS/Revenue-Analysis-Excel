# 📊 Executive Revenue Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive and executive-level Excel dashboard designed to analyze organizational revenue, profitability, product performance, and sales channels across multi-region operations (2016–2019).

## 📈 Key Metrics & Highlights (KPIs)

* 💵 **Total Revenue:** $7.43M *(Best Year: 2017 | Best Region: North America)*
* 💸 **Total Cost:** $23.8M *(Cost Ratio: 42% | Highest Category: Computers)*
* 💰 **Total Profit:** $32.45M *(Profit Margin: 57% | Best Channel: Store)*
* 📦 **Total Quantity Sold:** 251,207 units *(Best Country: United States | Best Manufacturer: Contoso, Ltd)*
* 🏷️ **Average Order Value (AOV):** $51,333 *(Best Month: May | Best Category: Computers)*

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **Regional Sales:** **North America** leads all sales regions with **$33.2M+**, followed by **Asia ($12.6M)** and **Europe ($10.4M)**.
2. **Sales Channels:** **Online sales** drive the largest share of revenue (**42%**), followed by **Resellers (26%)**, **Catalog (18%)**, and **Physical Stores (14%)**.
3. **Product Performance:** **Computers** and **Cameras & Camcorders** are the top-performing product categories in both revenue and average order value.
4. **Manufacturer Dominance:** **Fabrikam, Inc.** and **Contoso, Ltd** represent the top two leading manufacturers by total revenue volume.
